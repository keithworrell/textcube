Letterforms

Font
Heavy fonts give each view more material to work with.
Fill the face
Stretch letters to the full square. Higher = more complete letters, less natural proportions.
Boldness
Thicken strokes. Sturdier, prints better, and fewer gaps, but counters (holes) close up.

Finish

Letter corner rounding
Softens the corners of each letter's outline. Too much and letters start to look melted.
Edge fillet
Rounds the ridges where two letters cross. Silky feel, but it trims a little off silhouettes.
Polish
Mesh smoothing passes. Removes voxel stair-steps; high values soften crisp detail.
